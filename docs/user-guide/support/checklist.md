# Checklist

A checklist breaks a ticket's work into steps you tick off as they're done. A ticket has at
most one. The customer never sees it.

## Adding one

In the checklist card on the ticket, choose **empty** or a checklist template and click
**Add checklist**. Templates are kept under **Support › Ticket tools › Templates**, one item per
line.

## Working with it

- Tick an item when it's done. Hovering over a ticked item shows who ticked it.
- Add items in the box at the bottom. Use the arrow to move one up, and × to remove it.
- **Remove the checklist** deletes it and its items.

### Items that follow another ticket

An item that names another ticket, such as `Wait for #1042`, isn't ticked by hand. It's done
when that ticket is closed, rejected or merged, and shows a green circle when it is. Only tickets
of this tenant count. An item naming a number that isn't one of ours stays open.

## Resolving or closing with open items

If you resolve or close a ticket while checklist items are still open, you're asked first. Choose
**Keep it open**, or **Resolve anyway** / **Close anyway**.

Once a ticket is closed, its checklist is kept as it was and can't be changed.
