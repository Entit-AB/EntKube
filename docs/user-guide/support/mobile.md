# On a phone

The queue and the ticket view work at phone width, in the phone's browser. There's no separate
app and no separate address: sign in as usual.

## The queue

On a narrow screen the queue keeps the columns that decide what to open next: **priority**,
**ticket**, **status**, and the **response** and **resolution** clocks. The ticket number and,
in a tenant-wide queue, the customer move into the ticket cell above the title. The **update**
and **escalation** columns appear from tablet width up. Swipe sideways if the table is still
wider than the screen.

The header's buttons and the customer filter wrap onto a second line instead of running off
the edge.

## A ticket

- The figures and the action buttons wrap to fit.
- The side panels (customer, tags and links, checklist, attachments, issues) follow **below** the
  history rather than beside it.
- The ticket tabs scroll sideways.
- Keyboard shortcuts need a keyboard, so on a phone you use the buttons. The highlighter works
  wherever the phone lets you select text.

## What isn't made for a phone

Administration screens (text modules, macros, templates, the knowledge base editor, issue
trackers) and reporting work on a phone but are laid out for a desk.
