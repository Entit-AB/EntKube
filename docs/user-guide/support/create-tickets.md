# Create tickets

Most tickets arrive on their own: by mail, from the customer portal, from monitoring or from a
customer's own ticket system. Use **New ticket** when a matter reaches you any other way: a
phone call (in or out), a Teams message, or somebody at your desk.

## Registering one

In the queue, click **New ticket** and fill in:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **How it came in** | Received call, outbound call, Teams or chat message, or in person. Recorded on the history. |
| **Customer**, **Application** | The application decides which agreement, and so which support hours, the ticket is measured against. Leave it empty if nobody knows yet. |
| **Reported by**, **Their e-mail** | Who raised it. The address is where the receipt and your replies go. |
| **Title**, **What they said** | The title is what the customer sees, and becomes the mail subject. |
| **Their priority** | The customer's proposal. It stands until somebody confirms it (§14.3). |
| **Reported at** | When the customer raised it, in Swedish time. For a call registered afterwards, enter when the call came in: the agreement's clocks count from the report, not from when it was typed in. It can't be set in the future. |
| **Tags** | Separated by spaces or commas. |

**Create** registers the ticket in the usual way: the customer gets a receipt, the rota assigns
it, and its clocks start, exactly as for a ticket that came in by mail.

## Templates

When the right-hand panel lists templates, pick one and click **Apply**. A template fills in
**only the fields that are still empty**, so it never overwrites what you've typed. To replace
a value with the template's, clear the field first and apply again. Templates are kept under
**Support › Ticket tools › Templates**.
