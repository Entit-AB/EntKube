# Dashboard

**Support › Tickets › Dashboard** shows your figures beside the tenant's:

| Figure | What it is |
|---|---|
| My open tickets | Tickets you hold that aren't closed, with the tenant's unassigned count beside it |
| My reminders due | Your tickets whose reminder has come due |
| Escalated | Your tickets, and all the tenant's, with a deadline missed or due within 10 minutes |
| First response today | The average time to the first response for tickets answered today, yours and the tenant's, counted inside the support window as §14.4 counts it |
| New tickets in the last 7 days | By channel |
| Reopened in the last 7 days | Tickets the customer didn't accept as resolved |
| What colleagues did lately | The latest entries on the tenant's tickets by people other than you |

The figures are worked out when you open the page. **Refresh** works them out again.
