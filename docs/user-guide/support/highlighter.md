# The highlighter

Mark the words in a ticket that matter, such as the error message in a customer's mail or the
step that fixed it, so the next person reading the ticket sees them first.

## Highlighting

1. In the ticket's **History**, select the words with the mouse. The selection must lie within one entry.
2. Above the history, click a colour after **Highlight the selection**.

The words keep that colour for everyone working the ticket. The count beside the colours
(**2 highlights**) opens a list of them, with who marked each. The **×** removes one.

## What a highlight is, and isn't

- **Agents only.** Customers never see highlights, in the portal or in mail.
- **The entry isn't changed.** The history is the record between us and the customer, and its
  entries are never edited. A highlight is kept beside the entry, not in it.
- **Only words that are in the entry** can be highlighted. If the same words occur twice in an
  entry, the first occurrence is coloured.
- **Older browsers** that can't colour text still show the highlights in the list. The page says
  when this applies.
