# Time accounting

Time spent on a ticket is billable work. It's what the hour bank is drawn against, and what the
customer validates on the monthly specification (§20).

## After a note

When you add a note, EntKube asks **Time spent on this?** under the actions. Enter the minutes,
choose the kind of work, adjust the description (it starts as the note's first line) and click
**Log**. Or click **Skip**: logging time is never required, and it's never asked when changing
many tickets at once.

The time is recorded as ending when you click **Log**, and as yours.

## Kinds of work

| Kind | Billed |
|---|---|
| Management | Against the hour bank, per started hour and work pass |
| First assessment | The first 30 minutes are free |
| Development (§15) | Outside the hour bank |
| Travel | At 50% |

## Logging by hand

**Log time** in the ticket's time card records a stretch with a start and an end, given in
Swedish time. Use it for work that didn't end in a note, or that you're logging afterwards. The
card shows what was worked beside what §13 bills, since the two aren't the same number.
