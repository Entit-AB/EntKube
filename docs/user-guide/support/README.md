# Support: the agent's manual

How to work EntKube's support queue: answering customers, keeping the agreement's clocks,
and organising the work around them.

EntKube's support module is the system of record for an application-management agreement.
Every ticket is measured against that agreement's response and resolution times, so a few
things work differently from a general helpdesk. These chapters say where.

## Basics

1. [Ticket basics](ticket-basics.md): what a ticket is, its statuses, priorities and clocks.
2. [Find tickets](find-tickets.md): the queue, its overviews, and a ticket's own address.
3. [Create tickets](create-tickets.md): registering a call or a message by hand, and templates.
4. [Work with tickets](work-with-tickets.md): answering, notes, assigning, reminders and
   following, alone or many tickets at once.
5. [Replying by mail](replying.md): reply, reply all and forward, in rich text.
6. [Notifications, absence and your calendar](notifications.md): the bell, out of office, who's on a ticket, and a calendar feed.
7. [Dashboard](dashboard.md): your figures beside the tenant's.
8. [Keyboard shortcuts and tabs](keyboard-shortcuts.md): working the queue from the keyboard.
9. [On a phone](mobile.md): the queue and a ticket on a small screen.

`docs/helpdesk.md` lists what Zammad has that EntKube deliberately doesn't, with the reasons: AI,
the caller log and chat, i-doit, S/MIME and PGP, two-factor authentication (the platform's own) and
UI languages. It also explains why there are no ticket groups: the tenant is the queue.
