# Notifications, absence and your calendar

## The bell

The bell in the top bar counts your unread support notifications from every tenant you belong
to. Click it to see the latest; click one to open its ticket and mark it read, or **Mark all
read**.

A notification is made from what happens on a ticket, however it happened: on screen, by mail,
through a connected system or through the API. Several things on one ticket in the same minute
make one line ("3 updates, last by anna"). You're never told about what you did yourself.

| Kind | When |
|---|---|
| New ticket | A ticket is registered |
| Update | Anything else is added to the history |
| Assigned to you | A ticket is handed to you |
| Reminder due | A reminder on a ticket comes due |
| Deadline missed or near | A response, resolution or update deadline falls due within 10 minutes, or has passed. Once per deadline. |

Read notifications disappear after a day, and unread ones after 90 days.

## Choosing what you hear about

**Support › Tickets › My notifications & absence** has a matrix. For each kind, tick which tickets
you want to hear about:

- **My tickets**: the ones you hold, and those of a colleague you're covering for.
- **Unassigned**: the ones nobody holds.
- **Followed**: the ones you [follow](work-with-tickets.md#following-a-ticket).
- **All tickets**: everything in the tenant.

The defaults: everything about your own tickets, new and escalating unassigned ones, and updates
on tickets you follow. **Reset to defaults** puts them back. Following a ticket still also mails
you a digest, as before.

## Out of office

Give the first and last day you're away, and optionally a substitute and a note. While you're
away:

- the support rota passes over you, so new tickets don't land in your inbox;
- your substitute hears about your tickets as if they were theirs, and sees them in their queue
  under **Covering for**;
- you still receive your own notifications.

Your substitute must be another member of the tenant. Everyone's recorded absences are listed, so
the team can see who's away.

## Who else is on a ticket

At the top of a ticket, *also here* names colleagues who have it open right now, with ✎ beside
anyone writing a note or a mail on it. It's a courtesy, not a lock: two people can still change
the same ticket.

## Your deadlines in your calendar

**Make a feed** on the same page gives you an address to subscribe to in Outlook, Google
Calendar or Apple Calendar. It contains your tickets' reminders and their response and resolution
deadlines, and refreshes as they change.

The address works like a password: anyone who has it sees your deadlines. It's shown only once.
**Reset the address** makes the old one stop working, and **Turn it off** removes the feed.
